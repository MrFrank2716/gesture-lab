How to hold it

Grip the phone like a handle, any way up. A slide is a fast sideways sweep, as if cutting across a table top. A chop is a fast downward strike from the elbow. The detector works out which way is down from gravity, so the grip does not matter. Keep a firm hold and clear space around you.
